## Topic 3: Simultaneous Equations
*Cambridge O-Level (4024) / IGCSE (0580) — Intermediate*

---

### 📘 Topic Concept

A pair of **simultaneous equations** is solved when both equations are satisfied by the same values of the unknowns.

**Method 1 — Elimination**
Multiply (and/or add/subtract) so that one variable cancels.

**Method 2 — Substitution**
Solve one equation for one variable, substitute into the other.

**Method 3 — Graphical**
The intersection point of the two graphs gives the solution.

**Linear / Quadratic Pairs**
For a system like
$$y=mx+c,\qquad y=ax^{2}+bx+c'$$
substitute the linear $y$ into the quadratic to obtain a quadratic in $x$; solve, then back-substitute.

**Number of solutions** for linear/quadratic systems
- Two intersections → two solution pairs.
- One intersection (tangent) → repeated solution.
- No intersections → no real solutions.

### ✅ Answer Key

**Q1.** Add the equations: $(3x+y)+(x-y)=11+(-3)\Rightarrow 4x=8\Rightarrow x=2$. Then $y=11-3(2)=5$.

$$\boxed{x=2,\;y=5}$$

---

**Q2.** Substitute: $4x+3(2x-1)=7\Rightarrow 4x+6x-3=7\Rightarrow 10x=10\Rightarrow x=1$. Then $y=2(1)-1=1$.

$$\boxed{x=1,\;y=1}$$

---

**Q3.** Multiply Eq.1 by $2$ and Eq.2 by $5$:
$4x+10y=36$, $\;15x-10y=40$. Add: $19x=76\Rightarrow x=4$. Then $2(4)+5y=18\Rightarrow y=2$.

$$\boxed{x=4,\;y=2}$$

---

**Q4.** From Eq.2: $y=2x-3$. Substitute: $5x+3(2x-3)=4\Rightarrow 11x-9=4\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{13}{11}$. Then $y=2\cdot\dfrac{13}{11}-3=\dfrac{26-33}{11}=-\dfrac{7}{11}$.

$$\boxed{x=\dfrac{13}{11},\;y=-\dfrac{7}{11}}$$

---

**Q5.** Let pencil $=p$ and eraser $=e$ (in dollars):

$$2p+3e=1.05,\qquad 5p+4e=2.10$$

Multiply first by $5$, second by $2$, then subtract:
$10p+15e=5.25$ and $10p+8e=4.20\Rightarrow 7e=1.05\Rightarrow e=0.15$.
Then $2p+3(0.15)=1.05\Rightarrow 2p=0.60\Rightarrow p=0.30$.

$$\boxed{\text{pencil}=\$0.30,\;\text{eraser}=\$0.15}$$

---

**Q6.** Substitute $y=x+1$ into $x^{2}+y^{2}=25$:

$$x^{2}+(x+1)^{2}=25\Rightarrow 2x^{2}+2x+1=25\Rightarrow x^{2}+x-12=0\Rightarrow(x+4)(x-3)=0$$

$x=-4\Rightarrow y=-3$;  $x=3\Rightarrow y=4$. Points: $\boxed{(-4,-3)\text{ and }(3,4)}$.

---

**Q7.** $x^{2}-3x+2=2x-2\Rightarrow x^{2}-5x+4=0\Rightarrow(x-1)(x-4)=0$.
$x=1\Rightarrow y=0$;  $x=4\Rightarrow y=6$.

$$\boxed{(1,0)\text{ and }(4,6)}$$

---

**(b)** Substitute: $3s+14=2s+28\Rightarrow s=14$. Then $m=42$. $\boxed{m=42,\;s=14}$.

**Q9.**
**(a)** $2(L+W)=80$ and $L=2W+4$.
**(b)** From the first: $L+W=40$. Substitute: $(2W+4)+W=40\Rightarrow 3W=36\Rightarrow W=12$. Then $L=2(12)+4=28$.

$$\boxed{W=12\,\text{m},\;L=28\,\text{m}}$$
