## Topic 5: Mensuration — Volume & Surface Area
*Cambridge O-Level (4024) / IGCSE (0580) — Intermediate*

### 📘 Topic Concept

**Cuboid** of dimensions $l\times w\times h$:

$$V=lwh,\qquad S=2(lw+lh+wh)$$

**Cube** of side $s$:

$$V=s^{3},\qquad S=6s^{2}$$

**Cylinder** (radius $r$, height $h$):

$$V=\pi r^{2}h,\qquad S=2\pi r^{2}+2\pi rh$$

**Cone** (radius $r$, slant $l$, height $h$):

$$V=\tfrac{1}{3}\pi r^{2}h,\qquad S=\pi r^{2}+\pi rl,\qquad l=\sqrt{r^{2}+h^{2}}$$

**Sphere** (radius $r$):

$$V=\tfrac{4}{3}\pi r^{3},\qquad S=4\pi r^{2}$$

**Pyramid** (base area $A$, height $h$):

$$V=\tfrac{1}{3}A h$$

**Prism** (cross-section area $A$, length $\ell$):

$$V=A\ell$$

**Similar Solids** with linear scale factor $k$:
- Areas scale by $k^{2}$.
- Volumes scale by $k^{3}$.

### ✅ Answer Key

**Q1.**
**(a)** $V=8\times 5\times 4=\boxed{160\,\text{cm}^{3}}$.
**(b)** $S=2(40+32+20)=\boxed{184\,\text{cm}^{2}}$.

---

**Q2.**
**(a)** $V=\dfrac{22}{7}\times 49\times 20=\boxed{3080\,\text{cm}^{3}}$.
**(b)** $S=2\cdot\dfrac{22}{7}\cdot 49+2\cdot\dfrac{22}{7}\cdot 7\cdot 20=308+880=\boxed{1188\,\text{cm}^{2}}$.

---

**Q3.**
**(a)** $l=\sqrt{6^{2}+8^{2}}=\sqrt{100}=\boxed{10\,\text{cm}}$.
**(b)** $V=\dfrac{1}{3}\pi(36)(8)=\boxed{96\pi\,\text{cm}^{3}}$.
**(c)** $S=\pi(36)+\pi(6)(10)=36\pi+60\pi=\boxed{96\pi\,\text{cm}^{2}}$.

---

**Q4.**
**(a)** $V=\dfrac{4}{3}\pi(9)^{3}=\dfrac{4}{3}\pi(729)=\boxed{972\pi\,\text{cm}^{3}}$.
**(b)** $S=4\pi(81)=324\pi\approx\boxed{1018\,\text{cm}^{2}}$ (3 s.f.).

---

**Q5.**
**(a)** Cylinder $V=\pi(25)(12)=300\pi$. Hemisphere $V=\dfrac{2}{3}\pi(125)=\dfrac{250}{3}\pi$.

$$V_{\text{total}}=300\pi+\dfrac{250}{3}\pi=\dfrac{1150}{3}\pi=\boxed{\dfrac{1150\pi}{3}\,\text{cm}^{3}}$$

**(b)** $\dfrac{1150\pi}{3}\approx\boxed{1204\,\text{cm}^{3}}$ (3 s.f.).
**(c)** Curved cylinder $=2\pi(5)(12)=120\pi$. Bottom disc $=\pi(25)=25\pi$. Hemisphere curved $=2\pi(25)=50\pi$.

$$S=120\pi+25\pi+50\pi=195\pi\approx\boxed{613\,\text{cm}^{2}}$$ (3 s.f.).

---

**Q6.**
**(a)** Half-diagonal of base $=\dfrac{10\sqrt{2}}{2}=5\sqrt{2}$. Then perpendicular height $h=\sqrt{13^{2}-(5\sqrt{2})^{2}}=\sqrt{169-50}=\sqrt{119}\approx\boxed{10.9\,\text{cm}}$.
**(b)** $V=\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot 100\cdot\sqrt{119}=\dfrac{100\sqrt{119}}{3}\approx\boxed{364\,\text{cm}^{3}}$ (3 s.f.).

---

**Q7.**
**(a)** $A=\dfrac{1}{2}(1+2)(25)=\boxed{37.5\,\text{m}^{2}}$.
**(b)** $V=37.5\times 10=\boxed{375\,\text{m}^{3}}$.
**(c)** Capacity $=375\times 1000=\boxed{375\,000\,\text{L}}$.

---

**Q8.**
**(a)** Surface area ratio $=3^{2}:5^{2}=\boxed{9:25}$.
**(b)** Volume ratio $=3^{3}:5^{3}=\boxed{27:125}$.

---

**Q9.** Equate volumes: $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi(6)^{3}=\pi(4)^{2}h\Rightarrow\dfrac{4}{3}\cdot 216=16h\Rightarrow 288=16h\Rightarrow h=18$.

$$\boxed{h=18\,\text{cm}}$$

---

**Q10.**
**(a)** $50\,\text{L}=0.050\,\text{m}^{3}$. Rate $=\boxed{0.050\,\text{m}^{3}/\text{min}}$.
**(b)** Cross-section $A=\pi(0.6)^{2}=0.36\pi\approx 1.131\,\text{m}^{2}$.
**(c)** Rate of rise $=\dfrac{0.050}{0.36\pi}\approx 0.04421$ m/min $=4.421$ cm/min $\approx\boxed{4.42\,\text{cm/min}}$ (3 s.f.).
**(d)** Time $=\dfrac{1\,\text{m}}{0.04421\,\text{m/min}}\approx\boxed{22.6\,\text{min}}$ (3 s.f.).
