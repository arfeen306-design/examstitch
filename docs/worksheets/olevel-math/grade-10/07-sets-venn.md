### 📘 Topic Concept

**Set notation**
- $\xi$ — universal set.
- $\varnothing$ or $\{\}$ — empty set.
- $A\cup B$ — **union** ("$A$ or $B$ or both").
- $A\cap B$ — **intersection** ("$A$ and $B$").
- $A'$ — **complement** of $A$ ("not in $A$").
- $A\subseteq B$ — $A$ is a subset of $B$.
- $n(A)$ — number of elements in $A$.

**Two-set identity**

$$n(A\cup B)=n(A)+n(B)-n(A\cap B)$$

**De Morgan's Laws**

$$(A\cup B)'=A'\cap B'\qquad (A\cap B)'=A'\cup B'$$

**Three-set inclusion–exclusion**

$$n(A\cup B\cup C)=n(A)+n(B)+n(C)-n(A\cap B)-n(A\cap C)-n(B\cap C)+n(A\cap B\cap C)$$

**Venn Diagrams** — closed regions inside the universal rectangle, one for each set; overlaps represent intersections; numbers placed in each region give counts.

### ✅ Answer Key

**Q1.**

**(c)** $A'=\boxed{\{1,3,5,7,9,11\}}=B$ here.

**(b)** $Q=\boxed{\{4,8,12,16,20\}}$.

**Q3.**
**(a)** Only Maths $=32-14=\boxed{18}$.
**(b)** Only Physics $=24-14=\boxed{10}$.
**(c)** Neither $=50-(18+10+14)=50-42=\boxed{8}$.

**Q4.** Sketches (descriptions):
**(a)** $A\cap B'$ — the part of $A$ outside $B$ (left crescent).
**(b)** $A'\cap B'$ — outside both circles, inside $\xi$.
**(c)** $(A\cup B)'$ — same as (b).
**(d)** $A'\cup B$ — everything except (only $A$); equivalently the right circle plus everything outside $A$.

**Q5.**
**(a)** $n(A\cup B)=42+35-18=\boxed{59}$.
**(b)** Exactly one $=n(A)+n(B)-2\cdot n(A\cap B)=42+35-36=\boxed{41}$.
**(c)** $n(A'\cap B')=80-59=\boxed{21}$.

**Q6.**
**(a)** $\boxed{A\cap B'}$.
**(b)** $\boxed{A\cap B}$.
**(c)** $\boxed{(A\cup B)'\;=\;A'\cap B'}$.
**(d)** $\boxed{(A\cup B)\setminus(A\cap B)\;=\;(A\cap B')\cup(A'\cap B)}$ — symmetric difference.
