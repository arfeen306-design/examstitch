### 📘 Topic Concept

**Index Laws**

$$a^{m}\cdot a^{n}=a^{m+n},\quad \dfrac{a^{m}}{a^{n}}=a^{m-n},\quad (a^{m})^{n}=a^{mn},\quad (ab)^{n}=a^{n}b^{n}$$

$$a^{0}=1\;(a\neq 0),\quad a^{-n}=\dfrac{1}{a^{n}},\quad a^{1/n}=\sqrt[n]{a},\quad a^{m/n}=\sqrt[n]{a^{m}}=(\sqrt[n]{a})^{m}$$

**Surds**
A *surd* is an irrational root such as $\sqrt{2}$ or $\sqrt[3]{5}$. Useful identities:

$$\sqrt{ab}=\sqrt{a}\cdot\sqrt{b},\qquad \sqrt{\dfrac{a}{b}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{b}},\qquad (\sqrt{a})^{2}=a$$

To **simplify** $\sqrt{n}$, factor out the largest perfect square: $\sqrt{72}=\sqrt{36\cdot 2}=6\sqrt{2}$.

**Rationalising the denominator**

$$\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{a}}\times\dfrac{\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{a}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{a}}{a}\qquad\dfrac{1}{a+\sqrt{b}}\times\dfrac{a-\sqrt{b}}{a-\sqrt{b}}=\dfrac{a-\sqrt{b}}{a^{2}-b}$$

**Standard Form** $a\times 10^{n}$ with $1\le a<10$ and $n\in\mathbb{Z}$. Useful for very large or very small numbers.

### ✅ Answer Key

**Q1.**
**(a)** $81^{3/4}=(81^{1/4})^{3}=3^{3}=\boxed{27}$.
**(b)** $16^{-1/2}=\dfrac{1}{16^{1/2}}=\dfrac{1}{4}=\boxed{0.25}$.

---

**Q2.**
**(a)** $\dfrac{x^{8}\cdot x^{-3}}{x^{2}}=x^{8+(-3)-2}=\boxed{x^{3}}$.
**(b)** $(2a^{3}b^{-1})^{4}=2^{4}a^{12}b^{-4}=\boxed{\dfrac{16a^{12}}{b^{4}}}$.

---

**Q3.**
**(a)** $\sqrt{200}=\sqrt{100\cdot 2}=\boxed{10\sqrt{2}}$.
**(b)** $\sqrt{18}=3\sqrt{2}$, $\sqrt{50}=5\sqrt{2}$, $\sqrt{8}=2\sqrt{2}$.

$$3\sqrt{2}+5\sqrt{2}-2\sqrt{2}=\boxed{6\sqrt{2}}$$

---

**Q4.**
**(a)** $\dfrac{6}{\sqrt{3}}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3}}=\dfrac{6\sqrt{3}}{3}=\boxed{2\sqrt{3}}$.
**(b)** Multiply by the conjugate $2-\sqrt{3}$:

$$\dfrac{1}{2+\sqrt{3}}\cdot\dfrac{2-\sqrt{3}}{2-\sqrt{3}}=\dfrac{2-\sqrt{3}}{4-3}=\boxed{2-\sqrt{3}}$$

---

**Q5.**
**(a)** $3^{x}=81=3^{4}\Rightarrow\boxed{x=4}$.
**(b)** $9^{x}=27\Rightarrow(3^{2})^{x}=3^{3}\Rightarrow 3^{2x}=3^{3}\Rightarrow\boxed{x=\dfrac{3}{2}}$.

---

**Q6.**
**(a)** $(3.2\times 4)\times 10^{5+(-3)}=12.8\times 10^{2}=\boxed{1.28\times 10^{3}}$.
**(b)** $\dfrac{6.0}{1.5}\times 10^{8-3}=4\times 10^{5}=\boxed{4.0\times 10^{5}}$.

---

**Q7.** Convert each to base $2$:
$2^{10}=2^{10},\;4^{5}=2^{10},\;8^{3}=2^{9},\;16^{2}=2^{8}$.

$$\boxed{16^{2}<8^{3}<2^{10}=4^{5}}$$

---

**Q8.** $\sqrt{75}=\sqrt{25\cdot 3}=5\sqrt{3}\Rightarrow\boxed{a=5}$.
$\sqrt{12}=2\sqrt{3}$. So $\sqrt{75}-\sqrt{12}=5\sqrt{3}-2\sqrt{3}=\boxed{3\sqrt{3}}$.

---

**Q9.**
**(a)** $s=\sqrt{50}=\boxed{5\sqrt{2}\;\text{cm}}$.
**(b)** Diagonal $d=s\sqrt{2}=5\sqrt{2}\cdot\sqrt{2}=\boxed{10\;\text{cm}}$.

---

**Q10.**
**(a)** Convert each base to a power of $2$:

$$8^{x}=2^{3x},\quad 4^{2x}=2^{4x},\quad 16^{x-1}=2^{4(x-1)}=2^{4x-4}$$

$$N=\dfrac{2^{3x}\cdot 2^{4x}}{2^{4x-4}}=2^{3x+4x-(4x-4)}=2^{3x+4}\;\checkmark$$

**(b)** $N=128=2^{7}$:

$$2^{3x+4}=2^{7}\Rightarrow 3x+4=7\Rightarrow\boxed{x=1}$$

**(c)** $x=0$: $N=2^{4}=\boxed{16}$.
