### 📘 Topic Concept

**Probability of an event $E$**:

$$P(E)=\dfrac{\text{number of favourable outcomes}}{\text{number of equally likely outcomes}},\qquad 0\le P(E)\le 1$$

**Complementary events**:

$$P(\text{not }E)=1-P(E)$$

**Mutually exclusive events** (cannot occur together):

$$P(A\text{ or }B)=P(A)+P(B)$$

**Independent events** (one does not affect the other):

$$P(A\text{ and }B)=P(A)\times P(B)$$

**Sample space** — list of all possible outcomes; useful for two-stage experiments.

**Tree diagrams** — multiply probabilities along a branch, add probabilities of branches that satisfy the event.

**Expected frequency** $=n\times P(E)$ (over $n$ trials).

**Q4.**

**Q5.**
**(a)** $6\times 6=36$ outcomes (listed in a sum table).
**(b)** Sum $7:\{(1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1)\}=6$. $P=\dfrac{6}{36}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{6}}$.
**(c)** Sum $\le 4:\{(1,1),(1,2),(1,3),(2,1),(2,2),(3,1)\}=6$. $P=\dfrac{6}{36}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{6}}$.

**Q6.** Total $=8$.
**(a)** Tree branches — first draw $R$ ($\tfrac{5}{8}$) or $B$ ($\tfrac{3}{8}$); second draw conditional on first.
**(b)** $P(RR)=\dfrac{5}{8}\times\dfrac{4}{7}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{14}}$.
**(c)** $P(\text{one each})=P(RB)+P(BR)=\dfrac{5}{8}\cdot\dfrac{3}{7}+\dfrac{3}{8}\cdot\dfrac{5}{7}=\dfrac{15+15}{56}=\boxed{\dfrac{15}{28}}$.

**(b)** Odd $=\{1,3,5,7\}$, $P=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Expected $=200\times\dfrac{1}{2}=\boxed{100}$.
**(c)** No — actual frequency may differ from expected because each spin is independent and outcomes vary; expected is the *average* over many trials, not a guarantee.

**Q9.**

**(b)** Plays football or tennis $=11+5+7=23$. $P=\boxed{\dfrac{23}{30}}$.
**(c)** Plays neither $=7$. $P=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{30}}$.

**Q10.** Total $=8$ pens.
**(a)** $P(G_{1})=\dfrac{3}{8}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{8}}$.
**(b)** Given first was green, $2$ green left from $7$: $P(G_{2}\mid G_{1})=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{7}}$.
**(c)** $P(GG)=\dfrac{3}{8}\times\dfrac{2}{7}=\boxed{\dfrac{6}{56}=\dfrac{3}{28}}$.
**(d)** $P(\text{at least one R})=1-P(GG)=1-\dfrac{3}{28}=\boxed{\dfrac{25}{28}}$.
