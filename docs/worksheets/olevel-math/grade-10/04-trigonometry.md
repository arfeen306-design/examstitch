### 📘 Topic Concept

For a right-angled triangle with reference angle $\theta$:

- **Opposite** ($O$) — side facing $\theta$.
- **Adjacent** ($A$) — side next to $\theta$ (not the hypotenuse).
- **Hypotenuse** ($H$) — side opposite the right angle.

**SOH–CAH–TOA**

$$\sin\theta=\dfrac{O}{H},\qquad \cos\theta=\dfrac{A}{H},\qquad \tan\theta=\dfrac{O}{A}$$

To **find a side**: pick the ratio that involves the known side and the unknown.
To **find an angle**: use the inverse trig functions $\sin^{-1}$, $\cos^{-1}$, $\tan^{-1}$.

**Pythagoras** still applies in any right-angled triangle:

$$a^{2}+b^{2}=c^{2}$$

**Special angles**

| $\theta$ | $\sin\theta$ | $\cos\theta$ | $\tan\theta$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $0^{\circ}$ | $0$ | $1$ | $0$ |
| $30^{\circ}$ | $\tfrac{1}{2}$ | $\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ | $\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$ |
| $45^{\circ}$ | $\tfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ | $\tfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ | $1$ |
| $60^{\circ}$ | $\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ | $\tfrac{1}{2}$ | $\sqrt{3}$ |
| $90^{\circ}$ | $1$ | $0$ | undefined |

**Angles of elevation / depression** are always measured from the horizontal.

**Question 10 — Investigation** *[8]*

### ✅ Answer Key

**Q1.** $\sin 35^{\circ}=\dfrac{7}{H}\Rightarrow H=\dfrac{7}{\sin 35^{\circ}}\approx\boxed{12.2\,\text{cm}}$ (3 s.f.).

---

**Q2.** $\cos 40^{\circ}=\dfrac{A}{25}\Rightarrow A=25\cos 40^{\circ}\approx\boxed{19.2\,\text{cm}}$ (3 s.f.).

---

**Q3.**
**(a)** $H=\sqrt{5^{2}+12^{2}}=\sqrt{169}=\boxed{13\,\text{cm}}$.
**(b)** $\tan\theta=\dfrac{5}{12}\Rightarrow\theta=\tan^{-1}(5/12)\approx\boxed{22.6^{\circ}}$ (1 d.p.).

---

**Q4.**
**(a)** $\cos\theta=\dfrac{1.5}{6}=0.25\Rightarrow\theta=\cos^{-1}(0.25)\approx\boxed{75.5^{\circ}}$ (1 d.p.).
**(b)** Height $=\sqrt{6^{2}-1.5^{2}}=\sqrt{36-2.25}=\sqrt{33.75}\approx\boxed{5.81\,\text{m}}$ (3 s.f.).

---

**Q5.**
**(a)** $\tan 32^{\circ}=\dfrac{h}{50}\Rightarrow h=50\tan 32^{\circ}\approx\boxed{31.2\,\text{m}}$ (3 s.f.).
**(b)** $\tan 50^{\circ}=\dfrac{31.24}{d}\Rightarrow d=\dfrac{31.24}{\tan 50^{\circ}}\approx\boxed{26.2\,\text{m}}$ (3 s.f.).

---

**Q6.**
**(a)** Distance $=\sqrt{8^{2}+6^{2}}=\sqrt{100}=\boxed{10\,\text{km}}$.
**(b)** Bearing $=\tan^{-1}(8/6)$ from north $=53.13^{\circ}\Rightarrow\boxed{053^{\circ}}$ (nearest degree).

---

**Q7.**
**(a)** $\tan 24^{\circ}=\dfrac{80}{d}\Rightarrow d=\dfrac{80}{\tan 24^{\circ}}\approx\boxed{180\,\text{m}}$ (3 s.f.).
**(b)** New distance $=180+50=230$ m. $\tan\theta=\dfrac{80}{230}\approx 0.3478\Rightarrow\theta\approx\boxed{19.2^{\circ}}$ (1 d.p.).

---

**Q8.**
**(a)** Half base $=6$ cm. Height $h=\sqrt{10^{2}-6^{2}}=\sqrt{64}=\boxed{8\,\text{cm}}$.
**(b)** Half angle: $\sin(\angle/2)=\dfrac{6}{10}=0.6\Rightarrow\angle/2=36.87^{\circ}\Rightarrow\angle BAC\approx\boxed{73.7^{\circ}}$ (1 d.p.).

---

**Q9.**
**(a)** $d=\sqrt{9^{2}+6^{2}}=\sqrt{117}=\boxed{3\sqrt{13}\,\text{cm}}$ (or $\approx 10.8$ cm).
**(b)** $\tan\theta=\dfrac{6}{9}=\dfrac{2}{3}\Rightarrow\theta\approx\boxed{33.7^{\circ}}$ (1 d.p.).

---

**Q10.**
**(a)** Angle from $X$'s bearing to $Y$'s bearing $=120^{\circ}-30^{\circ}=\boxed{90^{\circ}}$. ✓

**(b)** Distances after $2$ h: $X$ is $40$ km from port, $Y$ is $30$ km from port. With angle $90^{\circ}$ between, by Pythagoras:

$$XY=\sqrt{40^{2}+30^{2}}=\sqrt{2500}=\boxed{50\,\text{km}}$$

**(c)** From $X$'s position: relative to north, the angle to $Y$ rotated through is $030^{\circ}$ (the back-bearing to port) plus the angle $\angle PXY$. With $\tan\angle PXY=\dfrac{30}{40}=0.75\Rightarrow\angle PXY\approx 36.87^{\circ}$.
Bearing of port from $X$ is $030^{\circ}+180^{\circ}=210^{\circ}$. Then $Y$ is east of this back-direction by $36.87^{\circ}$, giving bearing $\approx 210^{\circ}-36.87^{\circ}\approx\boxed{173^{\circ}}$ (nearest degree).
