### 📘 Topic Concept

A **quadratic equation** has the form $ax^{2}+bx+c=0$ where $a\neq 0$.

**Method 1 — Factorising**
Find two numbers $p$ and $q$ such that $p\cdot q=ac$ and $p+q=b$. Split the middle term and factor by grouping (for $a=1$ this reduces to $x^{2}+bx+c=(x+p)(x+q)$).

**Method 2 — Quadratic Formula**

$$x=\dfrac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^{2}-4ac}}{2a}$$

**Method 3 — Completing the Square**

$$ax^{2}+bx+c=a\left(x+\dfrac{b}{2a}\right)^{2}+c-\dfrac{b^{2}}{4a}$$

**Discriminant** $\Delta=b^{2}-4ac$:
- $\Delta>0$ → two distinct real roots,
- $\Delta=0$ → one repeated real root,
- $\Delta<0$ → no real roots.

**Sum and Product of Roots** for $ax^{2}+bx+c=0$:

$$\alpha+\beta=-\dfrac{b}{a},\qquad \alpha\beta=\dfrac{c}{a}$$

**The Parabola** $y=ax^{2}+bx+c$
- Opens upward if $a>0$, downward if $a<0$.
- Vertex (turning point) at $x=-\dfrac{b}{2a}$.
- $y$-intercept: $c$.

### ✅ Answer Key

**Q1.**
**(a)** $(x+3)(x+4)$.   **(b)** $(x-5)(x+2)$.   **(c)** $(2x-1)(x+3)$.

**Q2.**
**(a)** $(x-2)(x-5)=0\Rightarrow\boxed{x=2\text{ or }x=5}$.
**(b)** $(3x-2)(x+1)=0\Rightarrow\boxed{x=\dfrac{2}{3}\text{ or }x=-1}$.

---

**Q3.** $a=2,\;b=-5,\;c=-4$. $\Delta=25+32=57$.

$$x=\dfrac{5\pm\sqrt{57}}{4}=\boxed{x\approx 3.14\text{ or }x\approx -0.64}$$

---

**Q4.**
**(a)** $x^{2}-6x+1=(x-3)^{2}-9+1=(x-3)^{2}-8$, so $\boxed{p=3,\;q=-8}$.
**(b)** $(x-3)^{2}=8\Rightarrow x-3=\pm 2\sqrt{2}\Rightarrow\boxed{x=3\pm 2\sqrt{2}}$.

---

**Q5.**
**(a)** $\Delta=64-8k=0\Rightarrow\boxed{k=8}$.
**(b)** $8x^{2}+8x+2=0\Rightarrow 4x^{2}+4x+1=0\Rightarrow(2x+1)^{2}=0\Rightarrow\boxed{x=-\dfrac{1}{2}}$.

---

**Q6.**
**(a)** Let the integers be $n$ and $n+1$. $n(n+1)=156\Rightarrow\boxed{n^{2}+n-156=0}$.
**(b)** $(n-12)(n+13)=0\Rightarrow n=12$ (positive). Integers: $\boxed{12\text{ and }13}$.

---

**Q7.** Sum $=-2+5=3$, product $=-2\cdot 5=-10$. So $b=-3,\;c=-10$:

$$\boxed{x^{2}-3x-10=0}$$

---

**Q8.**
**(a)** Vertex at $x=-\dfrac{b}{2a}=-\dfrac{-4}{2}=2$. $y(2)=4-8-5=-9$. $\boxed{(2,-9)}$.
**(b)** $x^{2}-4x-5=(x-5)(x+1)=0\Rightarrow\boxed{x=5\text{ or }x=-1}$.

---

**Q9.**
**(a)** $x(x+3)=40\Rightarrow\boxed{x^{2}+3x-40=0}$.
**(b)** $(x-5)(x+8)=0\Rightarrow x=5$ (positive). Length $=5+3=8$. Dimensions $=\boxed{5\,\text{m}\times 8\,\text{m}}$.

---

**Q10.**
**(a)** At $t=0$, $h=1.5$ m. $\boxed{1.5\,\text{m}}$.

**(b)** $h=-5t^{2}+10t+1.5$. Vertex at $t=-\dfrac{10}{-10}=1$ s.

$$h_{\max}=-5(1)^{2}+10(1)+1.5=\boxed{6.5\,\text{m at }t=1\,\text{s}}$$

**(c)** Set $h=0$: $-5t^{2}+10t+1.5=0\Rightarrow 5t^{2}-10t-1.5=0\Rightarrow 10t^{2}-20t-3=0$.

$$t=\dfrac{20\pm\sqrt{400+120}}{20}=\dfrac{20\pm\sqrt{520}}{20}\approx\dfrac{20\pm 22.80}{20}$$

Take positive root: $t\approx\dfrac{42.80}{20}=\boxed{2.14\,\text{s}}$ (2 d.p.).
