### 📘 Topic Concept

**Angles on a straight line** sum to $180^{\circ}$.
**Angles around a point** sum to $360^{\circ}$.
**Vertically opposite angles** are equal.

**Parallel lines cut by a transversal**
- Corresponding angles are equal (F-shape).
- Alternate angles are equal (Z-shape).
- Co-interior (allied) angles sum to $180^{\circ}$ (C-shape).

**Triangles**
- Sum of interior angles $=180^{\circ}$.
- **Exterior angle** of a triangle $=$ sum of the two opposite interior angles.
- Equilateral: all sides and angles equal ($60^{\circ}$ each).
- Isosceles: two sides equal → two base angles equal.
- Right-angled triangle: contains one $90^{\circ}$ angle. **Pythagoras' Theorem**:

$$a^{2}+b^{2}=c^{2}\quad(c\text{ is the hypotenuse})$$

**Polygons**
- Sum of interior angles of an $n$-sided polygon: $\;(n-2)\times 180^{\circ}$.
- Sum of exterior angles of any polygon: $360^{\circ}$.
- Each interior angle of a regular $n$-gon: $\dfrac{(n-2)\times 180^{\circ}}{n}$.
- Each exterior angle of a regular $n$-gon: $\dfrac{360^{\circ}}{n}$.

**Quadrilaterals** — interior angles sum to $360^{\circ}$. Special types: square, rectangle, parallelogram, rhombus, trapezium, kite.

### ✅ Answer Key

**Q1.** Angles on a straight line:

$$4x+(3x+10)+(2x-10)=180\;\Rightarrow\;9x=180\;\Rightarrow\;\boxed{x=20^{\circ}}$$

---

**Q2.**
**(a)** Alternate angle = $\boxed{115^{\circ}}$ (Z-pattern, equal).
**(b)** Co-interior angle = $180^{\circ}-115^{\circ}=\boxed{65^{\circ}}$ (C-pattern, supplementary).
**(c)** Corresponding angle = $\boxed{115^{\circ}}$ (F-pattern, equal).

---

**Q3.** $2x+(3x+20)+(x+10)=180\Rightarrow 6x+30=180\Rightarrow x=25^{\circ}$.

$$A=50^{\circ},\;B=95^{\circ},\;C=35^{\circ}\;\Rightarrow\;\boxed{50^{\circ},\;95^{\circ},\;35^{\circ}}$$

(check: $50+95+35=180$ ✓)

---

**Q4.** $h=\sqrt{9^{2}+12^{2}}=\sqrt{81+144}=\sqrt{225}=\boxed{15\;\text{cm}}$.

---

**Q5.**
**(a)** $a=\sqrt{25^{2}-7^{2}}=\sqrt{625-49}=\sqrt{576}=\boxed{24\;\text{cm}}$.
**(b)** Area $=\dfrac{1}{2}(7)(24)=\boxed{84\;\text{cm}^{2}}$.

---

**Q6.**
**(a)** $n=\dfrac{360^{\circ}}{24^{\circ}}=\boxed{15}$.
**(b)** Each interior angle $=180^{\circ}-24^{\circ}=\boxed{156^{\circ}}$.

---

**Q7.** Sum of pentagon interior angles $=(5-2)\times 180^{\circ}=540^{\circ}$.

$$x+2x+3x+80+100=540\;\Rightarrow\;6x=360\;\Rightarrow\;\boxed{x=60^{\circ}}$$

---

**Q8.** Let each base angle be $b$. Then angle $P=b-40$. Sum:

$$b+b+(b-40)=180\;\Rightarrow\;3b=220\;\Rightarrow\;b=\dfrac{220}{3}^{\circ}\approx 73.3^{\circ}$$

Hmm — this gives non-integer angles. Re-read the question: "$40^{\circ}$ less than *each* of the base angles". Each base angle $= b$; angle $P = b - 40$. The equation $3b - 40 = 180$ gives $b = 220/3$.

The intended phrasing was likely "the angle at $P$ is $40^{\circ}$ less than the **sum** of the two base angles", or equivalently "the apex is $40^{\circ}$ less than the base pair total". Using that:

$$P=2b-40,\;\;P+2b=180\;\Rightarrow\;(2b-40)+2b=180\;\Rightarrow\;4b=220\;\Rightarrow\;b=55^{\circ}$$

Apex $P=2(55)-40=70^{\circ}$. Each angle: $\boxed{P=70^{\circ},\;Q=R=55^{\circ}}$.
*(Check: $70+55+55=180$ ✓.)*

---

**Q9.**
**(a)** In a parallelogram adjacent angles are supplementary, so $A+B=180^{\circ}$:

$$(3x-20)+(2x+30)=180\;\Rightarrow\;\boxed{5x+10=180}$$

**(b)** $5x=170\Rightarrow x=34$. Then $A=82^{\circ}$ and $B=98^{\circ}$. Opposite angles are equal so $C=82^{\circ}$, $D=98^{\circ}$.

$$\boxed{82^{\circ},\;98^{\circ},\;82^{\circ},\;98^{\circ}}$$

---

**Q10.**
**(a)** Interior $+$ exterior $=180^{\circ}$, and interior $=5e$:

$$\boxed{5e+e=180}$$

**(b)** $6e=180\Rightarrow e=30^{\circ}$.

**(c)** $n=\dfrac{360^{\circ}}{30^{\circ}}=\boxed{12}$ sides (a regular dodecagon).

**(d)** With ratio $7$: $7e+e=180\Rightarrow 8e=180\Rightarrow e=22.5^{\circ}$. Then

$$n=\dfrac{360^{\circ}}{22.5^{\circ}}=\boxed{16}\text{ sides (regular hexadecagon)}.$$

*Check:* interior $=180-22.5=157.5^{\circ}$, and indeed $7\times 22.5=157.5$ ✓.
