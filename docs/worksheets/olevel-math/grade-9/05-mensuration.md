### 📘 Topic Concept

**Perimeter** — total distance around the boundary of a 2D shape.

**Area Formulas**

$$\text{Rectangle}=l\times w\qquad\text{Square}=s^{2}\qquad\text{Triangle}=\tfrac{1}{2}\times b\times h$$

$$\text{Parallelogram}=b\times h\qquad\text{Trapezium}=\tfrac{1}{2}(a+b)h$$

**Circle** (radius $r$, diameter $d=2r$):

$$\text{Circumference}=2\pi r=\pi d\qquad\text{Area}=\pi r^{2}$$

**Sectors** (angle $\theta$ at the centre, in degrees):

$$\text{Arc length}=\frac{\theta}{360}\times 2\pi r\qquad\text{Sector area}=\frac{\theta}{360}\times\pi r^{2}$$

**Compound shapes** — split into rectangles, triangles, and circle parts; sum the pieces.

**Units**
- Length: mm, cm, m, km. ($1\,\text{m}=100\,\text{cm}$.)
- Area: mm$^{2}$, cm$^{2}$, m$^{2}$. ($1\,\text{m}^{2}=10\,000\,\text{cm}^{2}$.)

### ✅ Answer Key

**Q1.**
**(a)** $P=2(14+9)=\boxed{46\;\text{cm}}$.
**(b)** $A=14\times 9=\boxed{126\;\text{cm}^{2}}$.

---

**Q2.** $A=\dfrac{1}{2}\times 12\times 7=\boxed{42\;\text{cm}^{2}}$.

---

**Q3.** $A=\dfrac{1}{2}(10+14)\times 6=\dfrac{1}{2}(24)(6)=\boxed{72\;\text{cm}^{2}}$.

---

**Q4.**
**(a)** $C=2\pi r=2\times\dfrac{22}{7}\times 7=\boxed{44\;\text{cm}}$.
**(b)** $A=\pi r^{2}=\dfrac{22}{7}\times 49=\boxed{154\;\text{cm}^{2}}$.

---

**Q5.** Diameter $80$ cm ⇒ radius $40$ cm.
**(a)** Curved part $=\pi r=40\pi$. Straight diameter $=80$.

$$P=40\pi+80=\boxed{(40\pi+80)\;\text{cm}}$$

**(b)** $A=\dfrac{1}{2}\pi r^{2}=\dfrac{1}{2}\pi(40)^{2}=800\pi\approx\boxed{2510\;\text{cm}^{2}}$ (3 s.f.).

---

**Q6.** Outer area $=80\times 50=4000\,\text{m}^{2}$.
Inner rectangle (after $2$ m strip on each edge): $76\times 46=3496\,\text{m}^{2}$.

$$A_{\text{path}}=4000-3496=\boxed{504\;\text{m}^{2}}$$

---

**Q7.**
**(a)** $s=\sqrt{144}=\boxed{12\;\text{m}}$.
**(b)** $P=4\times 12=\boxed{48\;\text{m}}$.
**(c)** Diagonal $d=\sqrt{12^{2}+12^{2}}=\sqrt{288}=\boxed{12\sqrt{2}\;\text{m}}$.

---

**Q8.**
**(a)** Arc $=\dfrac{72}{360}\times 2\pi(10)=\dfrac{1}{5}\times 20\pi=4\pi\approx\boxed{12.6\;\text{cm}}$ (3 s.f.).
**(b)** Area $=\dfrac{72}{360}\times\pi(10)^{2}=\dfrac{1}{5}\times 100\pi=20\pi\approx\boxed{62.8\;\text{cm}^{2}}$ (3 s.f.).

---

**Q9.**
**(a)** Rectangle on the left, semicircle on the right with diameter equal to the rectangle's width.
**(b)** Perimeter $=$ three sides of the rectangle $+$ semicircle arc $=20+8+20+\dfrac{1}{2}(2\pi\cdot 4)=48+4\pi\approx\boxed{60.6\;\text{cm}}$ (3 s.f.).
**(c)** Area $=20\times 8+\dfrac{1}{2}\pi(4)^{2}=160+8\pi\approx\boxed{185\;\text{cm}^{2}}$ (3 s.f.).

---

**Q10.**
**(a)** Perimeter $=2(\text{length}+\text{width})=60$ ⇒ width $=30-x$. So width $=\boxed{(30-x)\,\text{m}}$.
**(b)** $A=x(30-x)$. ✓
**(c)** Tabulating selected integer values:

| $x$ | $30-x$ | $A=x(30-x)$ |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 25 | 125 |
| 10 | 20 | 200 |
| 14 | 16 | 224 |
| 15 | 15 | 225 |
| 16 | 14 | 224 |
| 20 | 10 | 200 |
| 25 | 5 | 125 |

Maximum occurs at $\boxed{x=15\,\text{m}}$, giving $A=\boxed{225\,\text{m}^{2}}$ (a square pen).
