### 📘 Topic Concept

**Algebraic Notation**
- A **variable** is a letter standing in for an unknown number — e.g. $x$, $y$, $a$.
- $3x$ means $3\times x$;  $x^{2}$ means $x\times x$;  $a^{2}b$ means $a\times a\times b$.

**Substitution**
Replace each letter by its numerical value, then evaluate following BIDMAS.

**Like Terms**
Like terms share **identical variables** to **identical powers**.

$$5x+3x=8x\quad\checkmark\qquad 5x+3x^{2}\quad\text{(already simplified)}$$

**Expanding Brackets**

$$a(b+c)=ab+ac\qquad (a+b)(c+d)=ac+ad+bc+bd$$

Standard product:

$$(x+a)(x+b)=x^{2}+(a+b)x+ab$$

**Factorising**
- *Common factor:*  $\;6x+9=3(2x+3)$
- *Quadratic (sum & product):*  $\;x^{2}+5x+6=(x+2)(x+3)$ — find numbers that **add** to $5$ and **multiply** to $6$.

**Solving Linear Equations**
Apply inverse operations to both sides until the variable is isolated.

**Linear Inequalities**
Same rules as equations **except** — multiplying or dividing both sides by a *negative* number **reverses** the inequality sign.

**Index Laws (in algebra)**

$$x^{a}\cdot x^{b}=x^{a+b},\qquad \frac{x^{a}}{x^{b}}=x^{a-b},\qquad (x^{a})^{b}=x^{ab},\qquad x^{-n}=\frac{1}{x^{n}}$$

**Simultaneous Equations (intro)**
Two unknowns need two equations. Solve by **elimination** (add or subtract to remove a variable) or **substitution** (express one in terms of the other).

### ✅ Answer Key

**Q1.**
**(a)** $2a+3b-c=2(3)+3(-2)-4=6-6-4=\boxed{-4}$.
**(b)** $a^{2}-bc=(3)^{2}-(-2)(4)=9-(-8)=\boxed{17}$.

**Q2.** Group like terms — $x$ terms then $y$ terms:

$$5x+2x-4x=3x,\qquad -3y+7y=4y$$

$$\therefore\;5x-3y+2x+7y-4x=\boxed{3x+4y}$$

**Q3.**

**Q4.**

$$x^{2}-9x+20=\boxed{(x-4)(x-5)}$$

**Q5.** Expand the bracket, then collect:

$$\Rightarrow\;3x+15-2x+6=24\;\Rightarrow\;x+21=24\;\Rightarrow\;\boxed{x=3}$$

**Q8.**
**(a)** $\dfrac{12a^{5}b^{3}}{4a^{2}b^{7}}=3a^{5-2}b^{3-7}=3a^{3}b^{-4}=\boxed{\dfrac{3a^{3}}{b^{4}}}$.

**(b)** First the bracket: $(2x^{3})^{4}=2^{4}x^{12}=16x^{12}$. Then divide:

**Q9.**
**(a)** Width $=w$ cm, length $=(2w+3)$ cm. Perimeter $=2\big(w+(2w+3)\big)=42$, i.e.

$$\boxed{6w+6=42}$$

**(b)** $6w=36\Rightarrow w=6$. Length $=2(6)+3=15$.

$$\therefore\;\text{dimensions}=\boxed{6\,\text{cm}\times 15\,\text{cm}}$$

**Q10.**
**(a)** With $x>y$:

$$x+y=24,\qquad 2x-y=33$$

**(b)** Add the two equations to eliminate $y$:

$$(x+y)+(2x-y)=24+33\;\Rightarrow\;3x=57\;\Rightarrow\;x=19$$

Substitute back: $y=24-19=5$.

$$\boxed{x=19,\;y=5}$$

**(c)** Check: $19+5=24$ ✓ and $2(19)-5=38-5=33$ ✓.
