### 📘 Topic Concept

**The Cartesian Plane**
- Two perpendicular axes — $x$-axis (horizontal) and $y$-axis (vertical) — meeting at the origin $O(0,0)$.
- A point is given by an ordered pair $(x,y)$ where $x$ is read first.
- Four **quadrants**: I $(+,+)$, II $(-,+)$, III $(-,-)$, IV $(+,-)$.

**Midpoint of two points** $A(x_1,y_1)$ and $B(x_2,y_2)$:

$$M=\left(\frac{x_1+x_2}{2},\;\frac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right)$$

**Distance / length** between two points (Pythagoras):

$$d=\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^{2}+(y_2-y_1)^{2}}$$

**Gradient (slope)** of the line joining two points:

$$m=\frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}}=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$$

**Equation of a straight line**

$$y=mx+c$$

where $m$ is the gradient and $c$ is the $y$-intercept.

**Parallel lines** share the same gradient.

**Intercepts**
- $x$-intercept: set $y=0$ and solve.
- $y$-intercept: set $x=0$ and solve.

### ✅ Answer Key

**Q1.**
**(a)** $Q(-4,3)$ has $x<0,\;y>0$, so it lies in **Quadrant II**.
**(b)** $M=\left(\dfrac{2+(-4)}{2},\dfrac{5+3}{2}\right)=\boxed{(-1,4)}$.

---

**Q2.**
**(a)** $m=\dfrac{8-2}{4-1}=\dfrac{6}{3}=\boxed{2}$.
**(b)** $|AB|=\sqrt{(4-1)^{2}+(8-2)^{2}}=\sqrt{9+36}=\sqrt{45}=\boxed{3\sqrt{5}}$.

---

**Q3.** $m=\dfrac{-7-5}{4-(-2)}=\dfrac{-12}{6}=\boxed{-2}$.

---

**Q4.**
**(a)** Using $y-y_1=m(x-x_1)$:

$$y-(-1)=3(x-2)\;\Rightarrow\;y+1=3x-6\;\Rightarrow\;\boxed{y=3x-7}$$

**(b)** $y$-intercept $=\boxed{-7}$.

---

**Q5.** Gradient $m=\dfrac{-3-5}{3-(-1)}=\dfrac{-8}{4}=-2$.

$$y-5=-2(x-(-1))\;\Rightarrow\;y-5=-2x-2\;\Rightarrow\;\boxed{y=-2x+3}$$

---

**Q6.**
**(a)** Rearrange the second equation: $4x-2y+7=0\Rightarrow 2y=4x+7\Rightarrow y=2x+\dfrac{7}{2}$.
Both lines have gradient $2$ → parallel. ✓
**(b)** $y$-intercepts: $-3$ and $\dfrac{7}{2}$.

---

**Q7.**
**(a)** $y=0$: $3x=12\Rightarrow x=4$ → intercept $\boxed{(4,0)}$.
**(b)** $x=0$: $2y=12\Rightarrow y=6$ → intercept $\boxed{(0,6)}$.
**(c)** Sketch — straight line joining $(4,0)$ on the $x$-axis and $(0,6)$ on the $y$-axis.

---

**Q8.**
**(a)** $\boxed{C=25+0.5d}$.
**(b)** $C=25+0.5(120)=25+60=\boxed{\$85}$.
**(c)** $95=25+0.5d\Rightarrow 0.5d=70\Rightarrow d=\boxed{140\;\text{km}}$.

---

**Q9.**
**(a)** $|AB|=\sqrt{6^{2}+0^{2}}=6$;
$\;|AC|=\sqrt{3^{2}+4^{2}}=\sqrt{25}=5$;
$\;|BC|=\sqrt{(3-6)^{2}+4^{2}}=\sqrt{9+16}=5$.
**(b)** Perimeter $=6+5+5=\boxed{16}$.

---

**Q10.**
**(a)** Gradients:

$$m_{PQ}=\frac{0-0}{4-0}=0,\quad m_{QR}=\frac{3-0}{5-4}=3,\quad m_{RS}=\frac{3-3}{1-5}=0,\quad m_{SP}=\frac{0-3}{0-1}=3$$

**(b)** $PQ\parallel RS$ (gradients $=0$) and $QR\parallel SP$ (gradients $=3$). Two pairs of parallel sides ⇒ $PQRS$ is a **parallelogram**. (It is *not* a rectangle because adjacent gradients $0$ and $3$ are not perpendicular.)

**(c)** $|PQ|=|RS|=4$;  $|QR|=|SP|=\sqrt{1^{2}+3^{2}}=\sqrt{10}$.

$$\text{Perimeter}=2(4)+2\sqrt{10}=\boxed{8+2\sqrt{10}}$$
