### 📘 Topic Concept

**Function notation**: $f(x)$ means "$f$ evaluated at $x$".
**Domain** — the set of inputs.   **Range** — the set of outputs.

**Composite functions**: $(fg)(x)=f(g(x))$ — apply $g$ first, then $f$.

**Inverse function** $f^{-1}$ undoes $f$:

$$f(f^{-1}(x))=x,\qquad f^{-1}(f(x))=x$$

To find $f^{-1}$: write $y=f(x)$, swap $x$ and $y$, and solve for $y$.

**Transformations of $y=f(x)$**

| Transformation | Effect |
|---|---|
| $y=f(x)+a$ | shift up by $a$ |
| $y=f(x-a)$ | shift right by $a$ |
| $y=-f(x)$ | reflect in the $x$-axis |
| $y=f(-x)$ | reflect in the $y$-axis |
| $y=kf(x)$ ($k>0$) | vertical stretch, factor $k$ |
| $y=f(kx)$ ($k>0$) | horizontal stretch, factor $\dfrac{1}{k}$ |

### ✅ Answer Key

**Q1.**
**(a)** $f(5)=2(5)-3=\boxed{7}$.
**(b)** $g(-2)=(-2)^{2}+1=\boxed{5}$.
**(c)** $g(3)=10$, then $f(10)=2(10)-3=\boxed{17}$.

---

**Q2.**
**(a)** $gf(x)=g\left(\dfrac{x+1}{2}\right)=3\cdot\dfrac{x+1}{2}-4=\dfrac{3(x+1)-8}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{3x-5}{2}}$.
**(b)** $fg(x)=f(3x-4)=\dfrac{(3x-4)+1}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{3x-3}{2}}$.

---

**Q3.** $y=\dfrac{2x-3}{5}\Rightarrow 5y=2x-3\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{5y+3}{2}$. Swap variables:

$$\boxed{f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{5x+3}{2}}$$

---

**Q4.**
**(a)** $y=\dfrac{x+4}{x-1}\Rightarrow y(x-1)=x+4\Rightarrow yx-y=x+4\Rightarrow x(y-1)=y+4\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{y+4}{y-1}$.

$$\boxed{f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{x+4}{x-1}}$$

(Note: $f$ is its own inverse.)
**(b)** Excluded value: $x=\boxed{1}$.

---

**Q5.**
**(a)** Shift up $5$: $\boxed{(2,2)}$.
**(b)** Shift right $1$: $\boxed{(3,-3)}$.
**(c)** Reflect in $x$-axis (negate $y$): $\boxed{(2,3)}$.
**(d)** Reflect in $y$-axis (negate $x$): $\boxed{(-2,-3)}$.

---

**Q6.**
**(a)** $y=x^{2}$, $x\ge 0\Rightarrow x=\sqrt{y}$. Swap: $\boxed{f^{-1}(x)=\sqrt{x}}$.
**(b)** Domain of $f^{-1}=$ range of $f=[0,\infty)$. Range of $f^{-1}=$ domain of $f=[0,\infty)$.

$$\boxed{\text{Domain}=[0,\infty),\;\text{Range}=[0,\infty)}$$

---

**Q7.** $f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{x-2}{3}$. Set $f(x)=f^{-1}(x)$:

$$3x+2=\dfrac{x-2}{3}\Rightarrow 9x+6=x-2\Rightarrow 8x=-8\Rightarrow\boxed{x=-1}$$

---

**Q8.**
**(a)** $ff(x)=f\left(\dfrac{1}{x-3}\right)=\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{1}{x-3}-3}=\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{1-3(x-3)}{x-3}}=\dfrac{x-3}{10-3x}$.

$$\boxed{ff(x)=\dfrac{x-3}{10-3x}}$$

**(b)** $\dfrac{1}{x-3}=2\Rightarrow x-3=\dfrac{1}{2}\Rightarrow x=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{2}}$.

---

**Q9.** Starting from $y=x^{2}$:
1. Shift right by $3$ → $y=(x-3)^{2}$.
2. Vertical stretch (factor $2$) → $y=2(x-3)^{2}$.
3. Shift down by $5$ → $y=2(x-3)^{2}-5$.

$$\boxed{\text{right }3,\;\text{vertical stretch }\times 2,\;\text{down }5}$$

---

**Q10.**
**(a)** $a+b=5$ and $3a+b=11$. Subtract: $2a=6\Rightarrow a=3$. Then $b=2$.

$$\boxed{a=3,\;b=2}$$

**(b)** $f(x)=3x+2\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{y-2}{3}\Rightarrow\boxed{f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{x-2}{3}}$.
