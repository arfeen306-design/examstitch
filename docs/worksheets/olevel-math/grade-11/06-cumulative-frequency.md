### 📘 Topic Concept

**Cumulative Frequency (CF)** — running total of frequencies up to (and including) the upper boundary of each class.

**Cumulative Frequency Curve (Ogive)** — plot CF against the **upper class boundary**; join the points with a smooth curve.

From an ogive (with $n$ data values):

$$\text{Median}=\text{value at }\dfrac{n}{2}\text{ on CF axis}$$

$$\text{Lower Quartile }(Q_{1})=\text{value at }\dfrac{n}{4}\qquad\text{Upper Quartile }(Q_{3})=\text{value at }\dfrac{3n}{4}$$

**Interquartile Range** $=Q_{3}-Q_{1}$.

**Box-and-Whisker Plot** — visual summary using minimum, $Q_{1}$, median, $Q_{3}$, maximum.

**Histogram with unequal class widths** uses **frequency density**:

$$\text{Frequency density}=\dfrac{\text{frequency}}{\text{class width}}$$

The **area** of each bar is proportional to the class frequency.

### ✅ Answer Key

**Q1.** Cumulative frequencies are $4,\;18,\;48,\;70,\;80$.

| Upper boundary | $20$ | $40$ | $60$ | $80$ | $100$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CF | $4$ | $18$ | $48$ | $70$ | $80$ |

---

**Q2.**
**(a)** Plot at $20,\;40,\;60,\;80,\;100$.
**(b)** $\dfrac{n}{2}=40$. From CF $=40$ (between $18$ and $48$): linear interpolation gives mark $\approx 40+\dfrac{40-18}{48-18}\times 20=40+\dfrac{22}{30}\times 20\approx\boxed{55}$.

---

**Q3.**
**(a)** $Q_{1}$ at CF $=20$: between $18$ and $48$. Mark $\approx 40+\dfrac{2}{30}\times 20\approx\boxed{41}$.
**(b)** $Q_{3}$ at CF $=60$: between $48$ and $70$. Mark $\approx 60+\dfrac{12}{22}\times 20\approx\boxed{71}$.
**(c)** IQR $\approx 71-41=\boxed{30}$.
**(d)** Students $>70$: at $70$, CF $\approx 60$ (interpolated similarly). Students above $70=80-60=\boxed{20}$.

---

**Q4.**
**(a)** Range $=82-20=\boxed{62}$.
**(b)** IQR $=60-35=\boxed{25}$.
**(c)** Median $48$ is closer to $Q_{1}\;(35)$ than to $Q_{3}\;(60)$, since $48-35=13$ vs $60-48=12$ — almost symmetric, very slightly **right-skewed** ($Q_{3}-\text{median}<\text{median}-Q_{1}$ does not hold strongly here, so the distribution is approximately symmetric).

---

**Q5.** Frequency density $=$ frequency $\div$ width:
$\dfrac{4}{2}=\boxed{2}$, $\dfrac{20}{4}=\boxed{5}$, $\dfrac{14}{2}=\boxed{7}$, $\dfrac{12}{6}=\boxed{2}$.

---

**Q6.** Frequency $=$ density $\times$ width:
$1.5\times 10+3.0\times 10+2.0\times 15+0.6\times 15=15+30+30+9=\boxed{84}$ people.

---

**Q7.**
**(a)** Densities: $\dfrac{25}{5}=5$, $\dfrac{80}{5}=16$, $\dfrac{55}{10}=5.5$, $\dfrac{25}{10}=2.5$, $\dfrac{15}{30}=0.5$.
**(b)** Highest density ($16$) → modal class $=\boxed{5\le t<10}$.
**(c)** Midpoints $2.5,7.5,15,25,45$: $\sum fx=25(2.5)+80(7.5)+55(15)+25(25)+15(45)=62.5+600+825+625+675=2787.5$. Mean $=\dfrac{2787.5}{200}\approx\boxed{13.9}$ min.

---

**Q8.**
**(a)** Test B (higher median $72$ vs $68$).
**(b)** Test A more consistent — smaller IQR ($14$ vs $22$).

---

**Q9.**
**(a)** $\boxed{50\%}$ (definition of IQR).
**(b)** $70-42=\boxed{28}$.
**(c)** Value $72$ is just above $Q_{3}=70$, so CF is just above $\dfrac{3}{4}\times 200=150$. Estimate $\approx\boxed{160}$.

---

**Q10.**
**(a)** $66-34=\boxed{32}$ students.
**(b)** Median at CF $=50$, between heights $160$ and $165$ (CF jumps $34\to 66$). Interpolate:

$$\text{median}\approx 160+\dfrac{50-34}{66-34}\times 5=160+\dfrac{16}{32}\times 5=\boxed{162.5\,\text{cm}}$$

**(c)** $Q_{1}$ at CF $=25$: between $14$ and $34$. $\approx 155+\dfrac{11}{20}\times 5\approx 157.75$.
$Q_{3}$ at CF $=75$: between $66$ and $86$. $\approx 165+\dfrac{9}{20}\times 5\approx 167.25$.
IQR $\approx 167.25-157.75\approx\boxed{9.5\,\text{cm}}$.

**(d)** CF at $172$: between $170$ ($86$) and $175$ ($96$). $\approx 86+\dfrac{2}{5}\times 10\approx 90$. So $100-90=10$ students $\Rightarrow\boxed{10\%}$.
