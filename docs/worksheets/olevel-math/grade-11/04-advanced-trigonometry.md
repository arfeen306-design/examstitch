### 📘 Topic Concept

For any triangle $ABC$ with sides $a$, $b$, $c$ opposite to vertices $A$, $B$, $C$:

**Sine Rule**

$$\dfrac{a}{\sin A}=\dfrac{b}{\sin B}=\dfrac{c}{\sin C}$$

Use when given:
- two angles and a side, or
- two sides and a non-included angle (watch for the *ambiguous case*).

**Cosine Rule**

$$a^{2}=b^{2}+c^{2}-2bc\cos A$$

Rearranged for an angle:

$$\cos A=\dfrac{b^{2}+c^{2}-a^{2}}{2bc}$$

Use when given:
- three sides, or
- two sides and the included angle.

**Area of a triangle**

$$\text{Area}=\tfrac{1}{2}ab\sin C$$

(use any two sides and the included angle).

**Bearings** — measured **clockwise** from north, given as a three-digit number (e.g. $075^{\circ}$).

**3D Trigonometry** — set up right-angled triangles within the 3D figure (e.g. through diagonals or perpendiculars).

### ✅ Answer Key

**Q1.** $\dfrac{8}{\sin 40^{\circ}}=\dfrac{10}{\sin B}\Rightarrow\sin B=\dfrac{10\sin 40^{\circ}}{8}\approx 0.8035\Rightarrow B\approx\boxed{53.5^{\circ}}$ (1 d.p.).

---

**Q2.** $PR^{2}=12^{2}+15^{2}-2(12)(15)\cos 110^{\circ}=144+225-360(-0.342)=369+123.1=492.1$.

$$PR=\sqrt{492.1}\approx\boxed{22.2\,\text{cm}}$$ (3 s.f.).

---

**Q3.** $\cos X=\dfrac{9^{2}+11^{2}-7^{2}}{2(9)(11)}=\dfrac{81+121-49}{198}=\dfrac{153}{198}\approx 0.7727$.

$$X=\cos^{-1}(0.7727)\approx\boxed{39.4^{\circ}}$$ (1 d.p.).

---

**Q4.**
**(a)** $BC^{2}=6^{2}+10^{2}-2(6)(10)\cos 72^{\circ}=36+100-120(0.309)=98.92$.

$$BC\approx\boxed{9.95\,\text{cm}}$$ (3 s.f.).

**(b)** Area $=\dfrac{1}{2}(6)(10)\sin 72^{\circ}=30(0.9511)\approx\boxed{28.5\,\text{cm}^{2}}$ (3 s.f.).

---

**Q5.**
**(a)** Bearing change $=150^{\circ}-60^{\circ}=90^{\circ}$. The interior angle at $Q$ is $180^{\circ}-90^{\circ}=90^{\circ}$ ✓.
**(b)** $PR=\sqrt{40^{2}+25^{2}}=\sqrt{2225}\approx\boxed{47.2\,\text{km}}$ (3 s.f.).
**(c)** $\tan(\angle\text{from }PQ)=\dfrac{25}{40}=0.625\Rightarrow$ angle $\approx 32^{\circ}$. Bearing of $R$ from $P=60+32=\boxed{092^{\circ}}$ (nearest degree).

---

**Q6.**
**(a)** $\angle L=180^{\circ}-35^{\circ}-80^{\circ}=\boxed{65^{\circ}}$.
**(b)** $\dfrac{LN}{\sin M}=\dfrac{LM}{\sin N}\Rightarrow LN=\dfrac{8\sin 35^{\circ}}{\sin 80^{\circ}}\approx\dfrac{4.589}{0.9848}\approx\boxed{4.66}$ (3 s.f.).
**(c)** Area $=\dfrac{1}{2}(8)(4.66)\sin 65^{\circ}\approx 18.64\times 0.9063\approx\boxed{16.9\,\text{cm}^{2}}$ (3 s.f.).

---

**Q7.**
**(a)** Largest angle is opposite the longest side ($250$ m):

$$\cos\theta=\dfrac{180^{2}+200^{2}-250^{2}}{2(180)(200)}=\dfrac{32400+40000-62500}{72000}=\dfrac{9900}{72000}\approx 0.1375$$

$\theta\approx\boxed{82.1^{\circ}}$ (1 d.p.).
**(b)** Area $=\dfrac{1}{2}(180)(200)\sin 82.1^{\circ}\approx 18000\times 0.9907\approx\boxed{17800\,\text{m}^{2}}$ (3 s.f.).

---

**Q8.**
**(a)** Diagonal $d=\sqrt{4^{2}+3^{2}+12^{2}}=\sqrt{169}=\boxed{13\,\text{cm}}$.
**(b)** Base diagonal $=\sqrt{16+9}=5$. Angle with base: $\tan\theta=\dfrac{12}{5}\Rightarrow\theta\approx\boxed{67.4^{\circ}}$ (1 d.p.).

---

**Q9.**
**(a)** Half-diagonal of base $=\dfrac{10\sqrt{2}}{2}=5\sqrt{2}\approx 7.071$. Height $h=\sqrt{13^{2}-(5\sqrt{2})^{2}}=\sqrt{169-50}=\sqrt{119}\approx\boxed{10.9\,\text{cm}}$.
**(b)** $\cos\theta=\dfrac{5\sqrt{2}}{13}\approx 0.5439\Rightarrow\theta\approx\boxed{57.0^{\circ}}$ (1 d.p.).

---

**Q10.**
**(a)** $\dfrac{6}{\sin 35^{\circ}}=\dfrac{8}{\sin\theta}\Rightarrow\sin\theta=\dfrac{8\sin 35^{\circ}}{6}\approx 0.7648$.

Acute: $\theta_{1}=\sin^{-1}(0.7648)\approx\boxed{49.9^{\circ}}$.
Obtuse: $\theta_{2}=180^{\circ}-49.9^{\circ}\approx\boxed{130.1^{\circ}}$.

**(b)** Third angle $=180-35-49.9\approx\boxed{95.1^{\circ}}$ (acute case) or $180-35-130.1\approx\boxed{14.9^{\circ}}$ (obtuse case).

**(c)** Two valid triangles — the *ambiguous case* of the sine rule.
